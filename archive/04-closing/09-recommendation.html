Recommendation 09
Recommendation

Make it a flagship, plug-and-play Superpower.

Three moves to turn Prompt Opinion's moat into a product customers feel.

01
Own the conversion layer.
Position end-to-end structured write-back as a core platform capability, and the foundation under every vertical — not just another agent in the registry.
02
Sell the moat as a product.
Integration and guardrails become something customers experience: plug-and-play, structured data on day one.
03
Keep extraction pluggable.
Point tools like PhenoML are swappable engines under the hood. Compete on the workflow, never the parser.
The extraction race is crowded. The connected, governed workflow is open — and it is exactly what Po already is.
Three concrete moves. The throughline: don't compete where the field is crowded; compete where Prompt Opinion already has unique distance — connected and governed.
