Beyond One Use Case

Every registry is the same problem in disguise.

Cancer, research, quality, public health, risk — each converts unstructured clinical text into a different structured schema. One engine sits under all of them.

Cancer registries
NAACCR / SEER
A shortage of certified tumor registrars leaves case-abstraction backlogs of 7–12 months.
Clinical research
CDISC / eCRF
Coordinators spend 2+ hours per patient, per day, retyping EHR data into trial systems.
Quality & registries
eCQM · NSQIP · NCDR
Health systems spend millions a year abstracting charts for CMS and dozens of registries.
Public health
eICR
Reportable conditions are still keyed and sent by hand — late and incomplete.
Risk adjustment
HCC / ICD-10
Manual chart review misses more than 40% of legitimate risk codes.
The Last Mile Layer
One conversion engine. Every vertical above runs the same workflow — ingest → extract → ground → reconcile → review → commit — pointed at a different target schema.
09 / 12
The five verticals are the proof that this is a foundational layer, not a single product. Each pain point is real and researched. Win one, the next is configuration, not a new build.
