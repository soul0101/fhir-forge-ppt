Who Does It

Everyone who touches the chart is doing data entry.

Different roles, different inputs — the same manual conversion, every day.

Intake Coordinator
Their input
Inbound faxes, referral packets and outside records.
Converts by
Re-typing them into the EHR, field by field.
Clinician
Their input
The patient encounter — everything just said and decided.
Converts by
Clicking it into problems, meds and orders — after hours.
Medical Coder / HIM
Their input
Finished clinical notes, waiting to be billed.
Converts by
Re-reading each one to assign ICD-10 and CPT codes.
Three roles, one job: turning unstructured information into structured data — by hand.
The role changes, the input changes, but the conversion work is the same. That's a pattern — and patterns are what software solves.
