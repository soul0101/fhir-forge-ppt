The Cost

Converting it by hand is slow, costly and fragile.

The manual conversion layer is invisible — until you add up what it takes.

Lost hours
Documentation runs late into the night — a leading driver of clinician burnout.
Real cost
Whole teams of coders, abstractors and data-entry staff exist to do this.
Errors creep in
Every re-keying risks a missed code, a duplicate problem, a stale medication.
Downstream breaks
Billing, quality measures, interoperability and AI all fail without structured data.
And it never scales — every new document, patient and data source adds cost in a straight line.
Frame the cost in four dimensions: time, money, accuracy, downstream impact. The point isn't any one of these — it's that all four scale linearly.
