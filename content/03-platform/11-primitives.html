Launch shapes Embedded in EHR · Standalone window
Four places the platform shows up that are worth a closer look. The brain works behind all of them. Every one of these inherits the same curated patient context the brain is already maintaining. Chart Copilot. The most familiar shape. An AI panel that lives next to whatever the clinician is looking at in the EHR. It reads the chart as they work and surfaces what matters in context. It doesn't get launched. It's always there. Signals. Every agent on the platform routes its output through here. Alerts, drafts, tasks, follow-ups. One queue for the clinician. Triage in one place. Instead of every AI tool inventing its own pop-up or notification scheme, they all land in the same Signals queue, routed to the right person. Line. Sometimes the touchpoint is a phone number. The doctor texts the on-call line and asks for an update on a patient overnight. The agent reads the chart, summarizes, cites its sources. Same idea as the Copilot, but the surface is a conversation, not a screen. SMART App. SMART-on-FHIR is the open protocol every major EHR already supports for launching apps inside the chart. Any builder can publish one. What makes it interesting on this platform is what the launched app gets for free. Standard SMART launch hands over the patient, the encounter, the user.
