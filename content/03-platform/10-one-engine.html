This is the architecture, and the answer to sprawl. Build it bottom-up. Integration first, because nothing else matters if you can't connect to every EHR and every source. Then the engine, the guardrails, and the provenance layer. Then the brain on top, continuously curated. And then the agents rise. Scribe, coding, prior auth, quality, trials, care management. Each one inherits the whole stack underneath. One PHI grant. One audit trail. One EHR pipe. Shared by every agent. Update the chart once. Every agent on top inherits the freshness.
