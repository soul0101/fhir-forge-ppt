Tone: this is about the *process*, not the people. The work itself is slow, fragmented, and re-keyed at every handoff. Three numbers in order: TIME (per encounter), LAG (before chart-ready), RE-KEYING (between handoffs). Then the flow diagram makes the chain visible — each "re-key" tag is a place errors creep in.
