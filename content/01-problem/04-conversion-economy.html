The Chart

The chart was built for billing. Not for AI.

It still does the original job. It does not work as the live source of truth that every agent above it has to act on.

01
Stale
Updates land during encounters. Between them, the chart falls behind reality.
02
Fragmented
Notes, faxes, outside records, labs, intake all live apart. Nothing reconciles them.
03
Mute
Each AI tool rebuilds patient context per query. Outputs rarely feed back into the chart.
The chart was built to record. It was never built to stay true.
03 / 11
Reframe: the pain is the chart, not the conversion labor. Three facets, each mapping to what the brain has to do later. Stale answers why a live layer matters. Fragmented answers why ingestion across every source matters. Mute answers why shared substrate + write-back matter. FHIR Forge is one capability inside the platform, not the centerpiece.
