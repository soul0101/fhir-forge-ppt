PhenoML is the most honest comparison — technically excellent at extraction, and stops there. Lang2FHIR is a one-way developer API: it never touches the patient's chart, never reconciles with existing data, never surfaces a review, never writes back. Five steps in the workflow; PhenoML covers one. Our pitch is the other four — the layer that turns a parser into a chart-aware loop.
