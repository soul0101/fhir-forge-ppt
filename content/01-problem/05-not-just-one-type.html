The slide's argument: conversion is one *kind* of work that appears in many disguises across healthcare. Read the column of TYPE badges out loud once — *FHIR-native, Flat File, Tabular, FHIR-emerging, CMS submission, Mixed.* That heterogeneity is exactly why a unified conversion engine has to exist as its own product.
