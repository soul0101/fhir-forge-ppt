This is the topology of the brain. In the middle, the brain. At the bottom, the chart. On top, the agents. And on the left, the things that feed the brain. The inputs are of two kinds. Some are structured. FHIR feeds, lab results, claims data. Some are unstructured. Notes, faxes, intake forms, portal messages. Both go in. The brain pulls them together into one picture of the patient. The chart at the bottom is kept in sync. The agents on top read from the brain when they need context. Their outputs feed back in, so the next agent has more to work with. One layer in the middle. Everything else connects through it. If asked how this is different from the chart alone: the chart mostly learns things at encounters. The brain takes in whatever lands, whenever it lands. An ED visit elsewhere, a referral fax, an outside lab. These can reach the brain before the next visit happens.
