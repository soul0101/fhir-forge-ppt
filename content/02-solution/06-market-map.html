Here's who's solving this today. Every real player on one picture. On the horizontal, how much of the workflow they own. On the vertical, how continuously they curate patient context. Bottom-left, the extraction APIs. Stateless. Cents per call. They lift entities out of text and disappear. No memory between calls. The lower band, autonomous coding and RCM. Fathom, CodaMetrix, AKASA, Datavant. Real depth in their lane, but their output goes to the claim, not back to the chart. The middle band, the ambient scribes. Abridge, Dragon Copilot, Suki, Ambience. They own the encounter, and nothing between encounters. Up and to the right, Innovaccer's Gravity is the closest competitor. Epic-native AI sits high on curation, but only inside Epic and only on Epic-shaped inputs. The top-right is genuinely empty. Continuously curated patient context, across every input, served to every agent. That's the brain.
