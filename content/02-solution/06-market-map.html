Datavant — HCC Complete (formerly Apixio)

Reveleer
Unified MA-payer platform — risk adjustment, quality (HEDIS), RADV audit, member management, and a new Clinical Data Repository (2026). 80+ customer organisations. Still payer-side.

Abridge
Ambient scribe with note + structured-field outputs. Deep Epic partnership (Workshop integration). Owns the encounter; nothing else.
Dragon Copilot

Iodine (Waystar)

Iodine Software (now Waystar)
AwareCDI suite — Concurrent, Retrospect, Forecast, Interact, Introspect. Surfaces CDI queries into the EHR for clinician acknowledgement. Now under Waystar / AltitudeAI™.
Regard

Regard

Epic-native AI

Walk the room across the chart, left-to-right then up-and-right: 1) Bottom-left: extraction primitives. Race to the bottom. Cents per call. 2) Mid-left: autonomous coding + HCC. Vertical-deep, but output ≠ chart. 3) Mid: ambient scribes. Own the encounter; not the rest of the chart. 4) Upper-mid: CDI / Regard. Real chart touch — narrow input. 5) Upper-left: Epic-native AI. Deep chart, only Epic, only Epic inputs. 6) Upper-right (Po target): end-to-end loop × governed chart writes. Then say it plain: "the top-right is genuinely empty — and that's where FHIR Forge points." Don't read every dot — let the audience hover. Honest caveat: positions are directional, not survey-grade. The relative placement is the argument; the absolute coordinates are not.
